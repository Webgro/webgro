Fewer visitors, considerably more money. The channel now earns £1.35 for every organic session against 59p a year ago. Two things drove that: the traffic that remains is far better qualified, and the site converts it more than twice as well.

Worth being clear-eyed about the trade. A shrinking session base is a shrinking pool of future customers, and there is a floor below which improved conversion stops compensating. Section 05 covers where that floor is.

[ The two-year picture ] Revenue

Trailing twelve-month revenue has nearly doubled.

Menswear is seasonal, so single months swing hard in both directions and say very little on their own. The trailing twelve-month total strips that out and shows the underlying trajectory: £53,776 at the November 2025 low, £114,997 at the end of August 2026.

02.

What actually changed.

Quality replaced volume. That is the whole story of the second year.

Product pages tell the clearest version of this.

Organic entrances landing straight on a product page nearly halved, and yet they now produce more than twice the orders and almost double the revenue. Conversion on those pages has gone from 0.49% to 2.01%.

Stock availability is visible in this data.

June 2025 recorded 1,223 organic entrances onto product pages and zero orders. A month of product-page traffic converting nothing points at a catalogue people could not buy from. As stock has been rebuilt, the same pages have converted progressively better, and the most recent quarter is the strongest on record. Search brought qualified demand to the door throughout; the difference now is that there is something behind it.

03.

Where the revenue lands.

Twelve months to August 2026, organic entrances by landing page.

Just under half of all organic revenue arrives on a homepage. The two homepages together took £54,620 of £114,999. Every collection page on the site combined contributed under £10,000.

Landing pageSessionsOrdersRevenueRev / session

A homepage-dominated organic profile means the channel is being fed by people searching the brand name. That is valuable and it is also finite: it grows only as fast as brand awareness does, and no amount of SEO work accelerates it much. Growth from here has to come from category pages, and those are currently contributing very little.

The opportunity, stated plainly.

If collection pages converted organic traffic at the rate the homepage does, and ranked where the brand's relevance says they should, the channel would roughly double again without a single additional brand search. The next section shows exactly which pages are closest to that.

05.

The link profile, and the clean-up.

A two-year decline, an active spam attack, and the blocking work now in place.

The trend

Aged links are dropping out faster than they are replaced

The profile has shed roughly 50 referring domains a month since late 2024, and the trend predates any deliberate clean-up. Authority Score has held steady at 31 to 32 throughout, so what is being lost is low quality.

The count matters less than the composition, but the direction still sets a ceiling on how far category pages can climb against better-linked competitors.

Active issue

A spam link campaign is being blocked

Since late June 2026, roughly 203 referring domains have appeared carrying anchor text advertising a PBN service, pointing at twistedtailor.com and at the Shelby & Sons and Noose & Monkey properties. Source domains include casino sites, PLR dumps and one adult site, all at zero page authority.

Blocking and disavow work has been running since May 2026. The attack is ongoing, so this stays an active workstream rather than a closed item.
